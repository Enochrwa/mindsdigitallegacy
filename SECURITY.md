# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not open a public issue for a security problem.

- Preferred: use GitHub's **Report a vulnerability** button on the repository's Security tab (private
  vulnerability reporting).
- Alternative: email `security@<our-domain>` (to be set once the company domain is registered).

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and the impact. We aim to acknowledge reports within
3 working days. Please give us a reasonable time to fix an issue before disclosing it.

## Scope

The public website, the API, and any MDL subdomain. Out of scope: denial-of-service testing, social
engineering of staff or learners, and third-party services (YouTube, email provider, hosting).

## Handling of personal data

Security incidents involving personal data are handled under Rwanda's Law No. 058/2021, including
notifying the National Cyber Security Authority within 48 hours of becoming aware of a breach.
See `docs/04-security-and-threat-model.md`.
